The story of Cartagena de Indias · 10.3910° N, 75.4794° W

Every wall has a pulse.

Cartagena is not a backdrop. It is a living Caribbean city shaped by encounter, ambition, violence, resistance, freedom and reinvention. Eight chapters — select any to read.

The whole story matters

Cartagena was a major colonial port in the transatlantic trafficking of enslaved Africans. Its wealth and monuments cannot be separated from that history. Neither can its extraordinary Afro-Caribbean legacy — a story of survival, freedom-making and cultural creation that remains alive across the city and region.
