A dark WooCommerce theme for Hotbox Office — cannabis & mushroom store. Black chassis,
champagne type, greenhouse-green accents.

== Description ==

Converted from the Hotbox Office single-page store into a full WordPress + WooCommerce
theme. Ships dark by default; a white/light scheme is one click away under
Appearance > Customize > Store Details > Color Scheme.

Features:

* Two color schemes — dark (black & champagne) and light (warm white & greenhouse
  green) — switchable in the Customizer; the hero wordmark panel stays dark in both
  as a brand chip
* Champagne announcement bar (editable in the Customizer) for the delivery promo
* Front-page hero with the greenhouse wordmark panel, "Elevate your mind." headline,
  and stats row
* Shop section with pill category chips (real WooCommerce product categories) and
  featured/latest products; a static catalog of the original 12 products shows until
  WooCommerce products exist
* Trust strip, reviews, FAQ (including delivery and payment answers), and visit/hours
  sections built in
* Full WooCommerce support: styled shop grid, single product, cart, checkout, account
  pages, AJAX header cart count, product-category chip filters on shop pages
* Customizer "Store Details" section: announcement text, hero kicker, address, phone,
  email, hours, and the footer disclaimer
* Custom logo support (falls back to the greenhouse SVG mark + wordmark)
* Footer menu and three footer widget areas; responsive with mobile menu toggle
* Translation-ready (text domain: hotbox-office)

== Installation ==

1. In WordPress admin, go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme.
2. Choose hotbox-office.zip and click Install Now, then Activate.
3. Install and activate WooCommerce, then add products in the four categories
   (Supplements, Coffee & Pantry, Grow Kits, Gourmet Dried — or your own). Mark up to
   eight as "featured" to pin them on the front page.
4. To recreate the store promo (15% off orders over $149), add a WooCommerce coupon or
   a pricing-rules plugin; the announcement bar text is editable under
   Appearance > Customize > Store Details.
5. For e-transfer and cash-on-delivery payments, enable WooCommerce's built-in
   "Direct bank transfer" (relabel it "Interac e-transfer") and "Cash on delivery"
   gateways under WooCommerce > Settings > Payments.
