Brand Concepts — Round Three — August 2026

JF Tire — Ten Logo Directions

Built around what the business actually is: commercial, fleet and trailer tire service in the GTA — mobile alignment, tandem axles, 24/7 roadside — not passenger-car retail. Round three replaces typed text with custom-drawn letterforms: a proprietary stencil-cut alphabet built on a 26-unit stem grid with a consistent 10-degree forward lean, drawn as raw vector geometry — no fonts anywhere in the primary marks. Strict white / black / red throughout, one red move per mark, and every lockup composed as a single unit rather than an icon beside a name.

01

Prime Wordmark

Flagship wordmark•Black / White / Red•01-prime-wordmark.svg

Custom-drawn letterforms, not a font: heavy stencil-cut caps built on a 26-unit stem grid with a 10-degree forward lean, sliced clean through by a speed line. This is the flagship — the name said once, said hard.

Where it works: Everything: signage, trucks, web, invoices. The anchor of the system.

02

Stacked Block

Vertical lockup•Black / White / Red•02-stacked-block.svg

The same drawn letters stacked square: JF over a red bar with tread sipes punched out of it, TIRE set full-width below. Reads at billboard distance and fits square placements the horizontal wordmark can't.

Where it works: Building signage, door decals, social profile, embroidery.

03

Rig Line

Illustrated lockup•Black / White / Red•03-rig-line.svg

The tractor-trailer silhouette rolling over a red passing line, with the drawn wordmark carrying the weight below. Icon and type set to the same width so the lockup holds as one block.

Where it works: Website header, trailer decals, quote sheets, yard signage.

04

Tile Monogram

App-icon tile•Black / White / Red•04-tile-monogram.svg

The drawn JF cut into a black rounded tile, with the F's crossbar flipped to red — one move, instantly ownable. Built to survive as a 32-pixel favicon or a fender badge.

Where it works: App icon, social avatar, favicon, embroidery, valve caps.

05

Budd Wheel Badge

Roundel•Black / White / Red•05-budd-wheel.svg

A 10-stud hub-piloted wheel reduced to a thin ring and its studs — the red one at 12 o'clock is a torque mark. The drawn JF sits in the hub. A detail only truck people clock, which is the point.

Where it works: Stickers, quality seal on quotes, floor mats, cap fronts.

06

Chevron Momentum

Dynamic wordmark•Black / White / Red•06-chevron-momentum.svg

Two drive-tread chevrons — black, then red — launching the drawn wordmark forward over a single red rule. The chevron pair alone is the favicon; the full lockup is truck-side livery.

Where it works: Truck-side livery, web, service portal, apparel.

07

24/7 Roadside Roundel

Service sub-mark•Black / White / Red•07-roadside-roundel.svg

The emergency-line badge with the numerals drawn in the same letter system — the red slash and the red segment at midnight, when the phone rings. Pairs with any primary mark.

Where it works: Service trucks, after-hours cards, invoice stamp.

08

True North Tread

Emblem•Black / White / Red•08-true-north.svg

A maple leaf with three steer-tire rib grooves through it, over the drawn wordmark. Owns the .ca in jftire.ca — unmistakably Canadian, unmistakably commercial.

Where it works: National fleet accounts, merch, trailer mudflaps, tenders.

09

Mudflap Stencil

Vertical mark•Black / White / Red•09-mudflap.svg

The logo as a mudflap in the drawn letters: white JF, red TIRE, and a red band carrying jftire.ca. The one that looks at home bolted behind a drive axle.

Where it works: Apparel, posters, real mudflaps, vertical door signage.

10

Fleetline Outline

Minimal B2B•Black / White / Red•10-fleetline-outline.svg

The same drawn wordmark, half solid, half outline — JF filled, TIRE stroked. A typographic move instead of an icon: quiet, confident, built for tenders and fleet service agreements.

Where it works: Fleet program proposals, service agreements, letterhead.
