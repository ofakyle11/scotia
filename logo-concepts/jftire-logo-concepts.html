Brand Concepts — Round One — August 2026

JF Tire — Ten Logo Directions

Built around what the business actually is: commercial, fleet and trailer tire service in the GTA — mobile alignment, tandem axles, 24/7 roadside — not passenger-car retail. Every mark below draws from truck hardware: deep-lug drive tread, tandem duals, 10-stud hub-piloted wheels, mudflaps, highway lines. From the earlier draft we kept what worked — the red/black/white palette and the italic forward momentum — and swapped the glossy car tire for iron that belongs in a truck yard.

01

Highway Hauler

Wordmark + rig•Red / Black / Steel•01-highway-hauler.svg

A conventional tractor pulling a van trailer — tandem duals, steer axle, exhaust stack — laying down a red tread track. Nothing about this reads passenger car: the silhouette is the customer's own truck. Keeps the heavy italic momentum and red/black split from the earlier draft.

Where it works: Website header, invoices, quote sheets, trailer decals.

02

Deep-Lug Shield

Badge / crest•Red / Black / White•02-deep-lug-shield.svg

A heraldic shield whose upper field is an actual drive-tire tread pattern — interlocking chevron lugs, the deep open tread you only see on truck rubber. Feels established and earned, like a fleet that's been on the road for decades.

Where it works: Uniform patches, caps, truck door decals, shop signage.

03

Tandem Stance

Icon + wordmark•Red / Black / Steel•03-tandem-stance.svg

A tandem drive axle seen side-on: two deep-lugged duals under a fender bar, leaving a red tread print. The tandem is the visual signature of a highway tractor — and it quietly nods to the multi-axle alignment work the shop is known for.

Where it works: Storefront signage, business cards, email signature.

04

Lug-Block Monogram

Monogram•Red / Black / Steel•04-lug-block-monogram.svg

The letters J and F built entirely out of tread blocks, leaning forward like a tire rolling into its contact patch. One red lug at the tip of the F gives it a signature. Stencil-industrial, like markings on a crate in a freight yard.

Where it works: Social avatar, merch, embroidery, wheel-chock stamps.

05

Budd Wheel Badge

Roundel•Red / Black / White•05-budd-wheel-badge.svg

A hub-piloted 10-stud wheel — the wheel every truck operator torques and every car driver has never counted. The tire's lugged shoulder forms the outer ring, circular type sits in the rim well, and the JF hub carries ten stud caps. A detail only truck people recognize, which is exactly the audience.

Where it works: Stickers, quality seal on quotes, valve caps, floor mats.

06

Chevron Drive

Dynamic wordmark•Red / Black / Steel•06-chevron-drive.svg

Three tread chevrons accelerating into the wordmark — steel, black, red — with a lug baseline under the type. The most modern and versatile of the ten: scales from a favicon (the three chevrons alone) up to full truck-side livery.

Where it works: Truck-side livery, web, app icon, service scheduling portal.

07

24/7 Roadside Roundel

Service sub-mark•Red / Black / Steel•07-roadside-roundel.svg

A companion mark rather than a replacement: the emergency-line badge for the 90-minute GTA roadside promise. The tire ring frames 24/7 with a red ROADSIDE plate. Pairs with any of the primary marks — this is the sticker a dispatcher slaps on the door of the service truck.

Where it works: Service trucks, after-hours cards, invoice stamp, fridge magnets for fleet managers.

08

True North Tread

Emblem•Red / Black / White•08-true-north-tread.svg

A maple leaf cut from rib tread — the grooves run straight through it like a steer tire's ribs. Owns the .ca in jftire.ca and plants the flag as a Canadian, GTA-based commercial tire company. Distinctive at any size and unmistakably not a US chain.

Where it works: National fleet accounts, merch, trailer mudflaps, tenders.

09

Mudflap Stencil

Vertical mark•Black / Red / Safety Yellow•09-mudflap-stencil.svg

The logo as a mudflap: hanger bar, bolts, stacked stencil type, and a safety-yellow hazard band. Shop-floor authenticity — this is the one that looks at home hanging behind a service counter or printed on an actual set of flaps.

Where it works: Apparel, posters, real mudflaps, vertical door signage.

10

Fleetline

Minimal B2B•Graphite / Steel Blue•10-fleetline.svg

The boardroom option. A tire horizon rising over a highway centreline, set in restrained spaced caps with a steel-blue accent instead of red. For the side of the business that wins fleet contracts: tenders, service agreements, national account proposals.

Where it works: Fleet program proposals, service agreements, letterhead.

Next step: pick one or two directions (or mix — e.g. Chevron Drive as the primary with the 24/7 Roundel as the service sub-mark) and we'll refine: custom-drawn letterforms instead of system type, full stationery lockups, and horizontal / stacked / one-colour variants.
