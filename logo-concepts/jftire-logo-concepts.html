Brand Concepts — Round Five — August 2026

JF Tire — Ten Logo Directions

Built around what the business actually is: commercial, fleet and trailer tire service in the GTA — mobile alignment, tandem axles, 24/7 roadside — not passenger-car retail. Round five fixes the thing that mattered: the letters. Every mark is now set in professional display type — Kanit Black Italic for the dynamic marks, Anton for the industrial ones — converted to pure vector outlines, so each logo is still a self-contained print-ready file with no font dependency. Strict white / black / red, one red move per mark, motion devices kept where they earn their place.

01

Apex Wordmark

Flagship wordmark•Black / White / Red•01-apex-wordmark.svg

Set in Kanit Black Italic and converted to pure vector outlines — real display type, professionally drawn, with a speed slice through the caps and a tapered red streak under the baseline. This is the flagship.

Where it works: Everything: signage, trucks, web, invoices. The anchor of the system.

02

Anton Stack

Vertical lockup•Black / White / Red•02-anton-stack.svg

The industrial route: Anton condensed caps stacked square — JF huge over a red sipe bar, TIRE tracked out to the exact same width. Zero tricks, maximum presence at distance.

Where it works: Building signage, door decals, social profile, embroidery.

03

Tread Shield

Badge / crest•Black / White / Red•03-tread-shield.svg

The solid shield with red drive-tread chevrons and Anton caps. Feels like it has been on a shop door for thirty years — in a good way.

Where it works: Uniform patches, caps, truck doors, shop signage.

04

Tile Monogram

App-icon tile•Black / White / Red•04-tile-monogram.svg

Kanit italic JF knocked out of a rounded black tile with a single slice through the letters. Built to hold up as a 32-pixel favicon or a fender badge.

Where it works: App icon, social avatar, favicon, embroidery, valve caps.

05

Budd Wheel Badge

Roundel•Black / White / Red•05-budd-wheel.svg

The 10-stud hub-piloted wheel — thin casing ring, ten studs, red torque mark at 12 o'clock — with Anton JF in the hub and TIRE tracked below. The truck-people detail, kept.

Where it works: Stickers, quality seal on quotes, floor mats, cap fronts.

06

Chevron Drive

Dynamic wordmark•Black / White / Red•06-chevron-drive.svg

Three drive-tread chevrons accelerating into the Kanit wordmark, red rumble strip fading out underneath. The chevron trio alone is the favicon.

Where it works: Truck-side livery, web, service portal, apparel.

07

24/7 Roadside Roundel

Service sub-mark•Black / White / Red•07-roadside-247.svg

The emergency badge with real drawn numerals — Kanit 24/7 with the slash in red, twin red ring segments at midnight and mid-day. Pairs with any primary mark.

Where it works: Service trucks, after-hours cards, invoice stamp.

08

True North Tread

Emblem•Black / White / Red•08-true-north.svg

The rib-tread maple leaf over Archivo Black — the sturdiest of the three faces — for the mark that carries the .ca. Unmistakably Canadian, unmistakably commercial.

Where it works: National fleet accounts, merch, trailer mudflaps, tenders.

09

Burnout Trail

Horizontal scene•Black / White / Red•09-burnout-trail.svg

A deep-lug tire laying twin fading skid marks that sweep under the Kanit wordmark. The cinematic one — built for a website hero or a trailer wrap.

Where it works: Website hero, vehicle wraps, banners, trade booth.

10

Grand Prix Plate

Plate lockup•Black / White / Red•10-grand-prix.svg

White Kanit italic on a leaning red plate — race-plate energy in one solid move. The strongest reversed treatment of the ten, and it prints in two colours.

Where it works: Truck doors, apparel, banners, sponsor walls.
