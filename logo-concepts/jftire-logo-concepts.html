Brand Concepts — Round Two — August 2026

JF Tire — Ten Logo Directions

Built around what the business actually is: commercial, fleet and trailer tire service in the GTA — mobile alignment, tandem axles, 24/7 roadside — not passenger-car retail. Round two tightens everything to the company colours — white, black and red, nothing else — with flatter geometry, upright modern type and a single red accent per mark. The truck DNA stays: drive-tread chevrons, tandem duals, the 10-stud hub-piloted wheel, the mudflap, the highway line.

01

Highway Hauler

Wordmark + rig•Black / White / Red•01-highway-hauler.svg

A conventional tractor and van trailer reduced to flat, rounded geometry over a single red centreline. No gradients, no clutter — the silhouette is the customer's own truck and nothing else.

Where it works: Website header, invoices, quote sheets, trailer decals.

02

Deep-Lug Shield

Badge / crest•Black / White / Red•02-deep-lug-shield.svg

One solid shield, one row of red drive-tread chevrons, the monogram, a red rule. Everything else from round one stripped away — it now holds up embroidered at one inch or painted on a door.

Where it works: Uniform patches, caps, truck door decals, shop signage.

03

Tandem Stance

Icon + wordmark•Black / White / Red•03-tandem-stance.svg

A tandem axle drawn as two clean rings with fine tread ticks and red hub points, grounded by a single road line that runs under the whole lockup. Quietly references the multi-axle alignment work.

Where it works: Storefront signage, business cards, email signature.

04

Lug-Block Monogram

Tile monogram•Black / White / Red•04-lug-block-monogram.svg

J and F built from tread blocks, now set in a rounded black tile — reads instantly as an app icon or avatar. One red lug at the tip of the F is the only accent.

Where it works: Social avatar, app icon, embroidery, merch.

05

Budd Wheel Badge

Roundel•Black / White / Red•05-budd-wheel-badge.svg

Stripped to the essentials of a 10-stud hub-piloted wheel: one thin casing ring, ten studs, the monogram. The single red stud at 12 o'clock is a torque mark — a detail every fleet tech will catch.

Where it works: Stickers, quality seal on quotes, valve caps, floor mats.

06

Chevron Drive

Dynamic wordmark•Black / White / Red•06-chevron-drive.svg

Two tread chevrons — black then red — driving into a tight, upright wordmark over a single red rule. The most versatile of the ten: the chevron pair alone works as a favicon.

Where it works: Truck-side livery, web, app icon, service portal.

07

24/7 Roadside Roundel

Service sub-mark•Black / White / Red•07-roadside-roundel.svg

The emergency-line badge reduced to a thin ring with a red segment at 12 o'clock — midnight, when the phone rings. Pairs with any primary mark as the sticker on the service truck door.

Where it works: Service trucks, after-hours cards, invoice stamp.

08

True North Tread

Emblem•Black / White / Red•08-true-north-tread.svg

A maple leaf with three clean rib grooves running through it — a steer tire's ribs. Owns the .ca in jftire.ca and reads Canadian at any size.

Where it works: National fleet accounts, merch, trailer mudflaps, tenders.

09

Mudflap Stencil

Vertical mark•Black / White / Red•09-mudflap-stencil.svg

The logo as a mudflap, now in strict company colours: black flap, white and red stacked type, and a single red band carrying jftire.ca. No hazard stripes, no hardware clutter.

Where it works: Apparel, posters, real mudflaps, vertical door signage.

10

Fleetline

Minimal B2B•Black / White / Red•10-fleetline.svg

The boardroom option, now on brand palette: a black tire horizon over a red highway centreline, spaced light caps against a heavy JF. For tenders and fleet service agreements.

Where it works: Fleet program proposals, service agreements, letterhead.

Next step: pick one or two directions (or mix — e.g. Chevron Drive as the primary with the 24/7 Roundel as the service sub-mark) and we'll refine: custom-drawn letterforms instead of system type, full stationery lockups, and horizontal / stacked / one-colour variants.
