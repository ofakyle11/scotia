Brand Concepts — Round Four — August 2026

JF Tire — Ten Logo Directions

Built around what the business actually is: commercial, fleet and trailer tire service in the GTA — mobile alignment, tandem axles, 24/7 roadside — not passenger-car retail. Round four opens the throttle. Same proprietary drawn alphabet — no fonts — now at a 14-degree lean with every motion device in the kit: speed slits cut through the caps, motion slivers arriving ahead of the letters, tapered ground streaks, fade-out skid marks, orbit arcs, slanted tread grooves and a lightning strike. Still strict white / black / red; the energy comes from geometry, not extra colours.

01

Velocity Wordmark

Flagship wordmark•Black / White / Red•01-velocity-wordmark.svg

The drawn letters pushed to a 14-degree lean with motion slivers arriving ahead of the J, two speed slits cut clean through the caps, and a tapered red ground streak accelerating under the name.

Where it works: Everything: signage, trucks, web, invoices. The anchor of the system.

02

Burnout Stack

Vertical lockup•Black / White / Red•02-burnout-stack.svg

The square lockup with the throttle open: JF sliced by a speed line over the red sipe bar, and a pair of skid marks fading in below — rubber laid by the mark itself.

Where it works: Building signage, door decals, social profile, apparel backs.

03

Hammer Rig

Illustrated lockup•Black / White / Red•03-hammer-rig.svg

The tractor-trailer now moving hard: tapered speed streaks trail off the back doors, the red passing line fades out behind it, and the sliced wordmark carries the momentum through.

Where it works: Website header, trailer decals, quote sheets, yard signage.

04

Ignition Tile

App-icon tile•Black / White / Red•04-ignition-tile.svg

The tile itself now leans with the letters — a parallelogram badge with the sliced JF and the red crossbar. Reads like a key fob for something with eighteen wheels.

Where it works: App icon, social avatar, favicon, embroidery, fender badges.

05

Orbit Budd

Roundel•Black / White / Red•05-orbit-budd.svg

The 10-stud wheel spun up: two red orbit arcs sweep around the casing showing rotation, the torque-marked stud holds 12 o'clock, and the sliced JF sits in the hub.

Where it works: Stickers, quality seal on quotes, floor mats, cap fronts.

06

Full-Send Chevrons

Dynamic wordmark•Black / White / Red•06-fullsend-chevrons.svg

Three drive-tread chevrons growing as they accelerate into the sliced wordmark, with the red underline fading out behind like a rumble strip in the mirror.

Where it works: Truck-side livery, web, service portal, apparel.

07

24/7 Strike Roundel

Service sub-mark•Black / White / Red•07-strike-roundel.svg

The emergency badge with a red lightning bolt drawn in the letter system splitting 24 from 7, and twin red segments on the ring at midnight and mid-day — round-the-clock, literally.

Where it works: Service trucks, after-hours cards, invoice stamp.

08

Northwind Leaf

Emblem•Black / White / Red•08-northwind-leaf.svg

The maple leaf at speed: rib grooves slanted to the same 14-degree lean as the letters, red slipstream streaks peeling off its left edge, sliced wordmark below.

Where it works: National fleet accounts, merch, trailer mudflaps, tenders.

09

Burnout Trail

Horizontal scene•Black / White / Red•09-burnout-trail.svg

A deep-lug tire at full lock laying twin skid marks that fade in from the left and sweep under nothing less than the whole lockup — the most cinematic of the ten.

Where it works: Website hero, vehicle wraps, banners, trade booth.

10

Apex

Minimal B2B•Black / White / Red•10-apex.svg

The half-solid, half-outline wordmark cut by a single red slash rising through the letters at full tilt — one aggressive move on an otherwise quiet mark, visible through the outlined TIRE.

Where it works: Fleet program proposals, service agreements, letterhead.
