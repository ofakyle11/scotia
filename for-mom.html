For my mother

Some of my favourite hours are the ones where you were sitting right behind me.

You got on. That’s the part I keep coming back to. You didn’t ask how fast it went or how long we’d be out there — you did up the life jacket, climbed on behind me, and held on.

You’ve done everything else the same way. Thank you for the patience I only understand now that I’ve needed it myself. For the meals that appeared without ever being asked for. For sitting up when you could have gone to bed. For believing in a version of me that hadn’t arrived yet, and waiting for it without once complaining.

I carry more of you than I ever tell you. The way I handle a hard day. The way I answer the phone. The way I can’t leave a room until I know everyone in it is all right — that one is yours.

I hope you know how loved you are. Not just today. Always.

With all my love,

Your son

P.S. — Next summer, same lake. I’ll drive slower. A little.
