The 28-Room Firm

Cinamate is twenty-eight rooms that share one project state, advise each other, and price everything against a published rate rather than a guess. The same architecture ports to a law practice almost room for room — a matter moves through phases the way a picture does, and every room downstream reads the same file.

What doesn't port is the tolerance for being wrong. A soft number in a budget is an estimate; a soft number in a filing is a sanction. Three rooms below are gates, not features.

01

What changes when the studio is a firm

Four things in the Cinamate architecture cannot survive the port unchanged. Everything else — one shared project state, rooms that advise each other, estimators anchored to published rates, mission control reading all of them — carries over intact.

Generation →

Verification

The generation floor's best trait is invention, and in law it is the failure mode. Every citation, quoted holding and statutory subsection is resolved against a real retrievable source and its current treatment before it can move to the next room. Unresolved means blocked, not flagged.

One project state →

Walled state

Cinamate wants every room to see the whole picture. A firm cannot let one client's facts surface in another client's brief. State is shared inside a matter and walled between them, enforced at the index rather than by instruction — because the wall is a professional obligation, not a preference.

Export →

Signature

A master ships when it renders. A filing ships when a licensed lawyer signs it, and the audit trail records what was drafted by the platform, what was edited, and by whom. The platform never files; it hands counsel something ready to file.

Location →

Jurisdiction

You can move a shoot. You cannot move the governing law. Jurisdiction is set at intake and constrains every room below it — limitation periods, pleading standard, page limits, interrogatory counts, and whether the case is in common law at all, because Québec is not.

Numbers you can defend

Cinamate's accuracy pitch is the part that ports best, because it is the part lawyers are hardest to sell without. No black boxes: every estimate traces to a published rate, a statute, or a fitted model — with the error bars shown.

ACC.01

Calibrated Outcomes

Recovery and exposure are modelled on reported verdicts and settlements in the claim type and the venue, and reported as bands because a range is what the data honestly supports. A point estimate on a case value is a number somebody will quote back to you in a deposition.

Published Scale, Not Guesses

Where Cinamate anchors to union rate cards, this anchors to instruments that are equally public and equally binding: filing fee schedules, statutory limitation periods, tariff and cost grids, statutory interest rates, lien formulas. Each entry carries its source and the date it was last confirmed.

Packaging

Cinamate's tiers map onto firm size almost directly, because the rooms a solo needs and the rooms a litigation boutique needs barely overlap.

Solo

Creator

One lawyer, no staff. Intake, conflicts, retainer, calendar, trust and books, mission control — the practice floor, plus the ethics gate.

8 rooms

Firm

Studio

Two to twenty lawyers. Adds research, drafting, filing, the court book and the client desk, across shared matters with real walls between them.

18 rooms

Litigation

Production

Adds the whole discovery phase and the red team: review, depositions, experts, exhibits, the moot room, trial book, waterfall.

26 rooms

Enterprise

Enterprise

All 28, plus on-premise or in-country deployment, data residency controls, insurer billing guidelines, and the audit surface a general counsel's office asks for.

28 rooms

Product architecture, not legal advice. Every room described here produces attorney work product for review by a licensed lawyer — the platform assists the practice of law and does not practise it. The jurisdictional details noted throughout are design constraints to build against, and each should be confirmed against current rules in the relevant jurisdiction before the room that depends on it ships.
